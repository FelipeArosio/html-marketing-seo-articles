What Makes a Wine Gift Box Special

Presentation and Materials

Rigid boxes, wooden cases, or premium cardboard protect and enhance the bottle. Details such as separators, ribbons, and personalized cards make the gift complete; on the shop, you can even add a message to include in the package.

Thoughtful Label Selection

A consistent theme makes the difference—region, grape, or style. Tasting Boxes offer coherent and convenient trios, while Gift Bottles include unique cases and Magnum formats with high visual impact.

How to Choose the Perfect Wine Gift Box

For White Wine Lovers

Choose fresh, precise selections suitable from aperitif to delicate dishes. Three-bottle boxes allow for educational comparisons between styles and regions—ideal for both beginners and enthusiasts.

For Red Wine Enthusiasts

For those who prefer elegant reds, silky-textured trios are perfect. Magnum formats in original boxes increase the gift’s value and allow for excellent bottle aging.

For Experts and Collectors

Vertical selections or prestigious vintages communicate attention and wine culture. Product sheets help verify vintage and tasting notes before purchasing.
