Tasting of the award-winning Donna Olimpia 1898 Bolgheri Bianco 2023

Color and aromas: clarity also recognized by critics

The color is bright straw yellow, while the nose shows citrus, white flowers, and mineral notes. A profile that international tasters have rewarded for precision and freshness, recognizing Donna Olimpia’s Bolgheri Bianco among the best labels of the appellation.

Palate and structure: a high-scoring white

On the palate, freshness leads the tasting. Sapidity accompanies the sip and the finish is long and balanced. Critics highlighted how the texture and progression make the wine suitable for refined food pairings, justifying the scores above 90 points it received.

A blend that convinces critics

The blend of Vermentino, Viognier, and Petit Manseng delivers balance between acidity, body, and honeyed nuances. The choice of different grape varieties has allowed the winery to obtain positive judgments and continuous accolades, placing Bolgheri Bianco among the Italian whites most followed by experts.

Food pairings with award-winning Bolgheri Bianco

Fish and shellfish: the award-winning freshness that enhances flavors

With raw seafood, oysters, and carpaccios of white fish, the wine shows the sapidity that convinced critics. Its crisp acidity elevates seafood dishes and underscores the elegance of the ingredients.

White meats and vegetarian dishes: versatility certified by awards

With delicate meats such as herb-roasted chicken or a lighter veal tonnato, Bolgheri Bianco maintains balance and harmony. Even Mediterranean vegetarian dishes find a perfect match in the wine, reinforcing the idea of versatility highlighted by international recognitions.
