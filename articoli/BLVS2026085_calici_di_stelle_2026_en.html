Calici di Stelle 2026: a national event combining wine, villages and the Night of San Lorenzo

The national event takes place between late July and mid-August, with the symbolic highlight of the Night of San Lorenzo on 10 August. Each participating municipality can, however, choose different dates, times and formats: some organise an evening in the town square, others create a multi-location route, while others offer a guided dinner or a winery tasting. For this reason, the official calendar and local pages should always be checked before purchasing a ticket or planning the journey.

Calici di Stelle 2026: what to expect during the evening

The programme may include tasting stands, local food products, music, guided tours, stargazing and moonlit walks. The common theme remains the opportunity to discover wines from the area, but the experience can vary considerably between a historic village, a winery open to the public and a city-wide festival. This variety is precisely what makes the event appealing even to those who are not wine experts and would like to approach tasting in an informal way.

Calici di Stelle 2026: how admission and wine tasting work

The most practical question is also the most frequently searched: is admission free or do you need to buy a ticket? The answer depends on the location, because Calici di Stelle 2026 is a national event made up of many local events.

Calici di Stelle 2026: free admission or tasting ticket?

In many locations, access to the event area may be free, while wine tasting requires a ticket. The ticket is usually needed to collect the official wine glass, the neck strap or glass holder and a specified number of tastings. Other events, such as dinners or guided tours, may instead require advance booking and have limited availability.

Calici di Stelle 2026: what the tasting ticket includes

The ticket may include a wine glass, coupons, tasting tokens and access to specific areas or activities. Food, tours, shows or shuttle services should not be assumed to be included: each local set of regulations specifies what is included and what must be paid for separately. At some events, wines are served only in the official glass purchased from the organisers' stands.

Calici di Stelle 2026: estimated prices and what to check before attending

Prices vary depending on the format: some routes offer inexpensive tasting kits, while others provide more complete packages. As a cautious guideline, tickets may often range from approximately €10 to €25, but there is no national price. Before setting off, it is worth checking advance sales, on-site purchases, the number of tastings, the time of the last admission and refund conditions in the event of bad weather.

Calici di Stelle 2026: key dates and times to note

The busiest period for Calici di Stelle 2026 is the one surrounding the Night of San Lorenzo, although many local programmes are spread over several days in order to manage visitor numbers, wineries and additional activities.

Calici di Stelle 2026: national period and the Night of San Lorenzo

The general period runs from late July to mid-August, peaking around 10 August. Some locations hold the event earlier, while others repeat it after Ferragosto, especially when the event is linked to a tourist itinerary or several evenings of wine tasting. In an updated article, the national date should always be accompanied by a recommendation to check the programme of the chosen municipality.

Calici di Stelle 2026: examples of locations already announced

Among the useful examples, Bolzano lists 7 August 2026, from 6:00 p.m. to 11:00 p.m., with tastings along the Portici and in Piazza Municipio. For Cirella, Colli Euganei/Monselice, Selci and other villages, the announced dates should be treated as local schedules to be confirmed on the organisers' official pages. This precaution helps avoid publishing outdated times if the programme is changed.

Calici di Stelle 2026: why opening times vary from one event to another

Some evenings begin at sunset, while others include dinner, entertainment or stargazing until late at night. Routes through historic villages may have tasting stands open for several hours, while winery visits often operate in scheduled time slots. The most useful advice for readers is to check the opening time, the final time for collecting the wine glass and the closing time of the tastings.

Calici di Stelle 2026: which wineries and municipalities are participating near me

Those searching for Calici di Stelle 2026 usually want to know where to go, not only what the event is. The best answer is to guide readers through the verification process, because participating wineries and municipalities may be added progressively.

Calici di Stelle 2026: how to find the nearest participating municipality

The first check should be made on the Movimento Turismo del Vino website and its associated regional pages. It is then advisable to check the website of the municipality, the local Pro Loco, the Strada del Vino or Città del Vino. Social media can be useful for quick updates, but it should not be the only source when checking times, tickets and bookings.

Calici di Stelle 2026: what to check on the municipality's page

  • The confirmed date and time of the event.
  • The exact location, parking facilities and any available shuttle services.
  • Participating wineries and wines available for tasting.
  • The ticket price, number of tastings and wine-glass collection procedure.
  • Mandatory booking requirements for dinners, tours or guided sessions.

Calici di Stelle 2026: village, winery or guided dinner?

A village event is the most sociable option, suitable for groups and leisurely walks. A winery event is better suited to those who want to learn more about the producer and the local area. A guided dinner, on the other hand, is the most gastronomic format and often requires advance booking. Choosing carefully beforehand helps ensure realistic expectations of the experience.

Calici di Stelle 2026: how to prepare for the wine tasting

A tasting evening works best when it is easy to manage: a few useful items, a clear programme and attention to the pace of the tastings.

Calici di Stelle 2026: what to bring with you

An identity document, booking confirmation or QR code, water, comfortable shoes and a light sweatshirt are often more useful than they may seem. At many events, bringing your own wine glass is unnecessary because the regulations may require the use of the official glass. It is also advisable to carry a card or cash after checking the accepted payment methods in advance.

Calici di Stelle 2026: how to taste wines without becoming confused

To follow the route more easily, you can start with white and rosé wines, continue with lighter red wines and leave the more structured wines until the end. Alternating water and small portions of food helps keep the palate more attentive. A quick note on your phone is enough to remember your favourite labels without turning the evening into an examination.

Calici di Stelle 2026: responsible consumption during the event

Calici di Stelle remains an event dedicated to enjoying wine, not to drinking large quantities. When drinking, it is best not to drive: shuttle services, taxis or a designated driver are practical options. Eating during the evening, drinking water and respecting your own limits make the experience more enjoyable and safer.

Calici di Stelle 2026: how to read the programme without missing information

A local programme may look simple, but it often contains several levels: admission, wine tasting, food, tours, concerts and shuttle services. Reading it carefully helps avoid surprises on arrival.

Calici di Stelle 2026: the difference between admission, wine tasting and dinner

Admission concerns access to the event area; wine tasting concerns the samples available with a wine glass and ticket; dinner is a gastronomic experience that may be separate or included only in specific packages. Guided tours and workshops, when available, often have limited places.

Calici di Stelle 2026: pay attention to sold-out events, time slots and advance sales

The most popular evenings may sell out before the event date, especially when they include dinner or a winery tour. Routes in town squares are often more flexible, but the number of tasting kits may still be limited. When the municipality offers advance sales, purchasing beforehand is usually the safest option.

Calici di Stelle 2026: families, groups and accessibility

For families and groups, it is useful to check food areas, toilets, accessibility, parking and activities that are not exclusively related to wine. Those attending with friends should organise transport and the return journey in advance, especially in villages where parking and shuttle services can have a significant impact on the experience.
