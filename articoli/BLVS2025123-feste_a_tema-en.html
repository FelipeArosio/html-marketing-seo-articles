Theme Parties: How to Host an Unforgettable Dinner

Why theme parties engage everyone

A theme party succeeds thanks to small rituals that involve guests: an appropriate dress code, a curated playlist, and mood lighting create the perfect frame. Plan the wine service as a three-act journey—aperitif, main courses, and finale—to pace the bottles and maintain energy and narrative tension without overload.

Horror theme: table, lighting, and details that matter

Set a dark tablescape using natural materials like wood and raw fabrics. Low candles and a deep red runner intensify the color in the glass, while matte tableware and simple risers for finger food keep the table clean and dramatic. A few well-chosen elements ensure functionality and consistency with the theme.
