Donna Olimpia 1898: Tuscan roots and a contemporary identity

Terroir and stylistic aim

On the Tuscan coast, the estate crafts wines with a saline streak and a clean, precise texture. The goal is to bottle aromatic precision and palate clarity: Vermentino Obizzo becomes the standard-bearer for Tuscany’s brighter side, with citrus aromas and a linear sip.

Winemaking approach and ageing

Obizzo 2024: the award-winning Vermentino

Sensory profile confirmed by sources

According to the official sheet and James Suckling’s review, Obizzo 2024 shows aromas of ginger, green apple, citrus, white spices and marine hints; on the palate you’ll find white peach, silky texture, crunchy acidity, and a refined finish. In short: a coastal, juicy, precise white to enjoy young.

When to drink it, what to pair it with, and how to serve it

Serve at 10–12 °C in a medium tulip glass. It works with raw seafood, shellfish, delicate risottos, chicken salads, and fresh cheeses. For aperitif, try Obizzo with oysters or amberjack tartare: the wine’s savoury trail enhances the sweetness of the fish.

What kind of wine is Bolgheri? ▶

Bolgheri DOC is an appellation on the Tuscan coast: whites are mainly Vermentino (sometimes with Viognier, Petit Manseng); reds are blends based on Cabernet/Merlot with other grapes possible. Style: Mediterranean profile, balanced structure, and excellent food versatility.
