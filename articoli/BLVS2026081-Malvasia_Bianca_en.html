Malvasia Bianca: pairings for aperitifs, fish and Mediterranean cuisine

At the table, Malvasia Bianca works best when the dish leaves room for its aromas. With taralli, green olives, tomato-topped friselle, vegetable crostini and fresh cheeses, its freshness and savouriness prepare the palate and make the wine easy to drink.

With fish, it is better to choose clean, simple preparations: sea bream, sea bass, prawns, squid, seafood salad and pasta dishes with seafood that are not overly intense. White meats with herbs, ricotta, fresh goat’s cheeses and light savoury pies can also enhance it, provided they do not contain excessively spicy sauces or strong smoky flavours.

Malvasia Bianca: how to drink and serve it

To enjoy Malvasia Bianca properly, the serving temperature should preserve its aromas. It should be served chilled, but not ice-cold: a temperature that is too low risks hiding the white flowers, acacia, broom and citrus notes that make it distinctive.

The ideal glass is a medium-sized white wine glass or a tulip-shaped glass. A narrow glass is unnecessary: the wine needs room to express its floral character and savouriness. Once purchased, it is a bottle to enjoy for its fragrance and drinkability, without necessarily waiting for a long ageing period.

FAQs about Malvasia Bianca

What are the characteristics of Malvasia Bianca?

Malvasia Bianca can produce fragrant, floral and fruity white wines. The 2023 Vigneti Reale Malvasia Bianca del Salento IGT stands out for its bright golden-yellow colour, aromas of white flowers, broom, acacia and citrus fruit, and a fresh, savoury, structured and persistent palate.

What type of wine is Malvasia?

Malvasia is not a single wine, but a family of grape varieties and styles. It can produce white, red, dry, sweet, still or sparkling wines. In this article, the reference is a Malvasia Bianca del Salento IGT: a fresh, savoury white wine from Puglia that is suitable for the table.

How should Malvasia be served?

Malvasia Bianca should be served chilled in a white wine glass or tulip-shaped glass, avoiding temperatures that are too low. It is suitable for aperitifs, fish dishes, white meats and fresh cheeses. For sweet, passito or sparkling versions, the specific product information should be checked.
