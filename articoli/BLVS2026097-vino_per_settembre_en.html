Wine for September: Why the Choice Changes After Summer

There is no need to abandon white wines when September arrives. What changes most are the ingredients and cooking methods: cold dishes are joined by risottos, roasted vegetables, cooked fish and white meats. A fresh wine still brings energy to the meal, while a medium body supports light sauces and creamier textures.

Acidity and structure are not opposites. Acidity prepares the palate for the next bite, while structure prevents the wine from disappearing alongside the dish. Serving also matters: a white wine served too cold loses aromatic expression, while a red left in a warm room can appear more alcoholic.

Wine to Drink in September: Freshness and Structure

A profile suited to the month combines tension, aromatic intensity and measured texture. Le Vallon 2022, meanwhile, is aged for ten months in stainless steel on the lees and offers greater roundness for more elaborate dishes.

The Best Autumn Wines: What Characteristics to Look For

The best autumn wines do not belong to a single category. Freshness and clearly defined aromas are useful for an aperitif, while body and persistence should be proportionate to the cooking method at the table. Savoury whites, medium-bodied reds and food-friendly sparkling wines can all find a place, provided that the pairing follows the dish.

An autumn white wine works well with fish, shellfish, goat’s cheeses, risottos and vegetables. A supple red is more suitable for cured meats, mushrooms and white meats than a highly tannic bottle. Colour alone does not determine the season: balance, temperature and intensity matter more.

Autumn White Wine: When It Works Best

The freshness of a Sauvignon Blanc can lighten a risotto, complement goat’s cheese and accompany baked fish. A simpler version prioritises immediacy and fragrance, while a selection aged on the lees develops greater texture. This difference helps distinguish between a wine for an aperitif and one suited to a complete dinner.

Le Vallon 2022: A More Complex Sancerre for September

Le Vallon 2022 is produced from a single vineyard covering almost three hectares, with vines described on the product page as approximately twenty-five years old. The soils include both caillottes and terres blanches. The grapes are harvested by hand, followed by gentle pressing and fermentation in stainless steel at a controlled temperature.

After fermentation, the wine ages for ten months in stainless steel on the lees. Contact with the fine lees can add volume, finesse and greater integration between the wine’s components without turning it into a sparkling wine. This fuller texture makes Le Vallon suitable for creamy risottos, baked fish, white meats with light sauces and more mature goat’s cheeses.

Wine for September and Ageing on the Lees

During ageing, the wine remains in contact with the yeast deposits left after fermentation. The producer controls this stage to obtain greater roundness and complexity. The result is not automatically superior to a standard Sancerre: it is simply better suited to dishes and occasions that require more depth.

The vintage indicates the year in which the grapes were harvested. The same producer’s Sancerre 2023 is described as showing white flowers, broom, lemon, grapefruit and hints of blackcurrant, with a taut, savoury texture. It ages on the fine lees for at least six months, gaining greater volume.

The 2024 vintage, meanwhile, focuses on fragrance, smoothness and a clearly defined profile of citrus, flowers and fresh fruit. Wine for September: Pairings with Autumn Dishes

Mushrooms, pumpkin, cheeses and baked dishes do not necessarily require a red wine. A standard Sancerre can accompany delicate mushrooms, vegetable risottos and seafood dishes. Le Vallon, meanwhile, has greater volume for creamy recipes or fish served with richer accompaniments.

With cheese, it is preferable to avoid very sweet preserves, which can make the wine seem more acidic. Fresh and moderately mature goat’s cheeses are more consistent with Sauvignon Blanc. Chicken and turkey with herbs or light sauces work well among white meats, while heavily spiced preparations require a different pairing.

With mushroom dishes, intensity and cooking method both matter. A delicate risotto can find balance in the freshness of Sancerre 2024, while a creamier preparation can be supported by the roundness of Le Vallon. Strongly toasted or bitter seasonings, however, risk overwhelming the wine’s profile.

Does the Grape Harvest Take Place in September?

Yes, September is one of the main harvest months in many Italian wine regions, but it is not the only one. Grapes used as bases for sparkling wines and some early-ripening varieties may be harvested between July and August, while late-ripening varieties can remain on the vines until October. The calendar varies according to region, altitude, climate, ripeness and the intended use of the grapes.

The decision does not depend solely on sugar levels. Technicians and producers also assess acidity, aromatic ripeness and the health of the grapes. The same variety can therefore be harvested several weeks apart according to the area and intended wine style.

2025 Grape Harvest: What Were the Official Forecasts?

The joint forecasts from Assoenologi, ISMEA and Unione Italiana Vini were published on 10 September 2025. They estimated Italian wine production at 47.4 million hectolitres, an increase of 8% compared with 2024 and close to the recent average.

The outlook indicated generally healthy grapes and quality prospects ranging from good to excellent in many areas, although there were regional differences. Southern Italy showed a stronger recovery, while Central Italy recorded an overall decline. These were still estimates subject to the weather conditions of the following weeks, not final production figures.

Quantity and quality must be considered separately: producing more hectolitres does not automatically result in better wine. Grape health, ripeness and work in the cellar determine the final result. These figures are also historical and should not be presented as current forecasts in 2026.
