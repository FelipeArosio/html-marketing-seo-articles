Nuragus di Cagliari: where to start to understand this Sardinian white wine for summer

What kind of wine is Nuragus and why it interests those looking for freshness

Nuragus is a white grape variety grown in Sardinia, particularly linked to the Cagliari and Campidano areas. In Nuragus di Cagliari DOC, the grape variety must form the base of the wine: regional sources indicate at least 85% Nuragus, with the possible presence of other authorised white grapes. It is therefore an identity-driven Sardinian white wine, not a simple generic label.

The 2025 version by Antonella Corda, offered on Vinoso.shop, is produced from 100% Nuragus grapes and comes from Serdiana, near Cagliari. Its interest for the reader is concrete: it offers a dry, slender and savoury profile, suitable for those who want a Sardinian white wine for summer capable of accompanying fish, appetisers and Mediterranean dishes without weighing them down.

Why Nuragus di Cagliari is a Sardinian white wine for summer

On hot days, a white wine must be easy to read, fresh and useful at the table. Nuragus di Cagliari responds well to this need because it combines aromatic delicacy, lively acidity and a saline note that recalls the Mediterranean context. It does not seek excessive volume: it works on precision and ease of pairing.

The Vinoso product page for Antonella Corda’s Nuragus mentions green apple, pear, citrus fruit, a marine note and a finish of aromatic herbs. These elements help explain why this wine can work with raw seafood, grilled fish, fresh cheeses and Sardinian appetisers. In other words, it is a white wine designed to prepare the palate and support clear, clean flavours.

Nuragus di Cagliari: aromas and taste of the Sardinian white wine for summer

Colour and aromas of Nuragus di Cagliari

In appearance, Nuragus di Cagliari is generally straw yellow, with possible greenish reflections. In the case of the Nuragus Antonella Corda 2025, Vinoso describes it as bright straw yellow, a detail consistent with the idea of a fresh and young white wine. The colour anticipates a direct style, suited to those who are not looking for an opulent wine but for a fragrant bottle.

On the nose, the profile is delicate: green apple, pear, citrus fruit and a saline sensation. These notes should not be read as a lack of intensity; they are instead the wine’s strength when paired with light dishes. A white wine that is too aromatic could overpower the food, while Nuragus remains measured and gastronomic.

Dry, savoury taste and clean finish

On the palate, the Nuragus di Cagliari DOC 2025 Antonella Corda is described as dry, slender and savoury, with persistent acidity and a fine structure. Ageing on the lees in stainless steel for six months helps give greater precision to the sip, without turning it into a heavy wine. The result is an agile white wine, but not an empty one.

This combination of freshness and savouriness makes Nuragus useful in many situations: aperitif, summer lunch, fish dinner, informal dining. The finish with aromatic herbs also helps connect it to Mediterranean cuisine, where basil, parsley, wild fennel and citrus fruits are often key ingredients.

Nuragus di Cagliari: pairings for a Sardinian white wine for summer

Aperitif, fish and Mediterranean cuisine

Nuragus di Cagliari works very well with light appetisers: olives, almonds, grilled vegetables, simple crostini, fresh cheeses and cold salads. Its acidity prepares the palate, while its savouriness accompanies salty ingredients without the need for excessive structure.

At the table, the most natural connection is with fish. Sea bream, sea bass, prawns, seafood salad, grilled fish and raw seafood are coherent pairings. Even a fregola with seafood, provided it is not too intense, can enhance the wine’s marine profile. To maintain balance, it is better to avoid heavy sauces and very spicy preparations.

Vegetables, fresh cheeses and dishes to avoid

In the vegetable world, Nuragus pairs well with courgettes, grilled aubergines, delicately prepared artichokes, aromatic herbs and summer savoury pies. With cheeses, it is better to stay with ricotta, fresh goat cheeses, primo sale or young pecorino, avoiding overly aggressive maturations.

It is not, however, the most suitable wine for braised meats, very flavourful red meats, concentrated sauces or sugary desserts. In those cases, Antonella Corda’s Cannonau or Cannonau Riserva may be more coherent choices. The point is not to create a hierarchy, but to choose the right bottle for the right dish.

Nuragus di Cagliari: how to serve the Sardinian white wine for summer

Temperature, glass and drinking occasion

Nuragus should be served chilled, but not ice-cold. A temperature that is too low risks hiding green apple, citrus fruit, aromatic herbs and the saline note. For the Antonella Corda Nuragus, the Vinoso product page suggests 8–10 °C: a useful range to enhance freshness and precision without muting the aromas.

The ideal glass is a white wine glass or tulip glass, not too narrow. This allows the wine to open up and the reader to better perceive its nuances. Since it is a white wine designed for freshness and fragrance, it is advisable to drink it young, following the vintage and availability indicated on the product page.
