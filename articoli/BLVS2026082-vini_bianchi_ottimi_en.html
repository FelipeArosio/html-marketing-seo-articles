Excellent white wines: the summer food-friendly white wine that makes the difference

Excellent white wines: why Fiano di Avellino is a summer food-friendly white wine

Making a good impression does not depend only on price. What matters is the consistency between the bottle, the occasion and the dishes. A summer food-friendly white wine must be fresh, but it must also be capable of accompanying food without disappearing. Fiano di Avellino DOCG works precisely because it combines acidity, savouriness, clearly defined aromas and a more substantial structure than many aperitif white wines.

In the case of the Laura De Vito Fiano wines available on Vinoso.shop, the strength lies in their origin in Lapio, one of the most recognisable areas of Irpinia. The three labels Li Sauruni, Arianiè and Verzare are made from Fiano grapes and express different vineyard parcels while maintaining an elegant profile that is highly suited to the table.

Excellent white wines: what to check before choosing

Before purchasing, it is worth carefully reading the denomination, vintage, producer, district, alcohol content, aromatic profile and recommended pairings. This quick check helps avoid choices that are unsuitable for the menu. A white wine may be excellent, but if the dish is too spicy, sweet or fatty, it risks losing precision in the glass.

Excellent white wines: characteristics of Fiano di Avellino DOCG

Excellent white wines: colour, aromas and flavour of a summer food-friendly white wine

Fiano di Avellino DOCG is a white wine from Campania made mainly from Fiano grapes. Production regulations require at least 85% of this variety, while the Laura De Vito labels listed by Vinoso are presented as 100% Fiano. In the glass, one can expect a straw-yellow white wine, often with bright reflections, and aromas of flowers, citrus fruit, white-fleshed fruit, aromatic herbs and, in more complex interpretations, a note of hazelnut or minerality.

On the palate, freshness prepares the mouth and savouriness supports the pairings. This balance explains why Fiano can be an elegant aperitif wine, but also a bottle suitable for an entire dinner. Its structure does not make it heavy: instead, it gives the wine depth, rhythm and persistence.

Excellent white wines: why Fiano can age with interest

Among Italian white wines, Fiano is also appreciated because it can age well when it comes from healthy grapes, a suitable terroir and careful winemaking. There is no need to promise longevity for every bottle: it is more accurate to say that the grape variety has a natural ability to develop more complex nuances while retaining energy and tension when the wine is well made.

Excellent white wines on Vinoso.shop: Li Sauruni, a summer food-friendly white wine with character

Excellent white wines: Laura De Vito Li Sauruni 2023

Li Sauruni 2023 is the broadest and most expansive Fiano di Avellino DOCG among the wines considered. It comes from the Sauroni district, known as “Li Sauruni”, in Lapio, from a vineyard located at approximately 600 metres above sea level. The Vinoso product page lists 100% Fiano, a 0.750 L bottle, 14.0% alcohol by volume and maturation in stainless steel for nine months, followed by bottle ageing.

Its sensory profile is rich but clearly defined: lime, bergamot, pear, apricot, white flowers, wild mint, basil, fresh hazelnut and a delicate smoky note. On the palate, it is broad, fresh, savoury and persistent, with a mineral finish. It is the bottle to choose when the menu includes shellfish, raw seafood, seafood risottos or baked fish.

Excellent white wines: Arianiè, the tense and mineral summer food-friendly white wine

Excellent white wines: Laura De Vito Arianiè 2023

Arianiè 2023 comes from the Arianiello district in Lapio, with vineyards located between 500 and 550 metres above sea level on clay soils containing volcanic ash and with a northern exposure. The Vinoso product page lists 100% Fiano, 13.5% alcohol by volume, a 0.750 L bottle and the Gambero Rosso Tre Bicchieri 2026 award. It is therefore a highly convincing choice for anyone wanting to serve a recognisable and award-winning white wine from Campania.

In the glass, it displays a more tense and vertical profile: grapefruit, apple, pear, white flowers, rosemary and mineral nuances. The palate is savoury, enveloping and long, with returning citrus notes and a clean finish. Arianiè is particularly suited to oysters, raw seafood, seafood pasta dishes and fresh cheeses, where precision and freshness matter more than power.

Excellent white wines: Verzare, the refined and terroir-driven summer food-friendly white wine

Excellent white wines: Laura De Vito Verzare 2023

Verzare 2023 completes the journey through Laura De Vito’s Fiano wines with a different interpretation of Lapio. The Vinoso product page presents it as a Fiano di Avellino DOCG from the Verzare district, at approximately 550 metres above sea level, with a northern exposure and mixed clay soils containing limestone. The bottle size is 0.750 L, the alcohol content is 13.0%, and the listed price is the same as the other two labels: €26.50.

Its profile is refined and terroir-driven: mandarin, pear, apple, medicinal herbs, white flowers and a mineral character. On the palate, it is full, enveloping, fresh and balanced, with returning citrus notes and a long, savoury finish. It pairs well with shellfish, seafood risottos, baked fish and comparative tastings of white wines from Campania.

  • Verzare: comparative tastings, delicate fish and Mediterranean cuisine.

Excellent white wines: pairings for a summer food-friendly white wine

Excellent white wines: aperitifs, seafood and summer dishes

A well-chosen Fiano di Avellino naturally accompanies refined aperitifs and seafood-based meals. It works well with oysters, amberjack tartare, raw prawns, marinated anchovies, linguine with clams, baked sea bream, sea bass and seafood risotto. Freshness prepares the palate, while savouriness and structure hold the dish together without overpowering it.

It is best to avoid very sugary desserts, excessively spicy sauces and very fatty red meats: these flavours risk hiding the citrus fruit, aromatic herbs, minerality and refinement. With delicate white meats, fresh cheeses and Mediterranean dishes that are not overly intense, however, Fiano remains bright and food-friendly.

Excellent white wines: how to serve a summer food-friendly white wine

Excellent white wines: temperature, glass and the right occasion

Serving these Fiano wines chilled but not ice-cold preserves their freshness without sacrificing their aromas. A temperature that is too low can close down the citrus fruit, white flowers, herbs and mineral notes; a temperature that is too high can make the palate feel less precise. The ideal glass is a medium-sized tulip-shaped white wine glass, capable of allowing the wine to breathe without dispersing its aromas.

After purchase, the bottles can be enjoyed for their energy and clarity, but they can also be stored carefully by those who want to observe how Fiano develops over time. The practical rule remains simple: read the product information, consider the menu and choose the bottle best suited to the occasion.
